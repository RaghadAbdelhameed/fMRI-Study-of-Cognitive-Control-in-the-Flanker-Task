Experimental Design

Task Structure & Conditions

The Flanker task probes cognitive control — your brain's ability to suppress irrelevant, interfering stimuli while focusing on the goal-relevant one.

Congruent Easy

Flanking arrows point in the same direction as the central arrow. No conflict — fast and accurate.

> > > > >
Correct response: Right ▶
Incongruent Hard

Flanking arrows point in the opposite direction. Conflict slows responses and increases errors.

> > < > >
Correct response: Left ◀

Trial Sequence Timeline

Each trial follows a fixed sequence with a jittered inter-trial interval — critical for separating BOLD responses in fMRI.

1
Fixation Cross
500 ms

2
Flanker Stimulus
2000 ms
5-arrow array presented. Subject presses left or right button. Either congruent (→→→→→) or incongruent (→→←→→) array shown.
3
Jittered ITI
8,000 – 14,000 ms (mean 12 s)
Inter-trial interval varies randomly (8,9,10,11,12,13,14 s). Jittering decorrelates the BOLD HRF across trials, enabling deconvolution in GLM analysis.

The BOLD Signal & HRF

fMRI measures the Blood-Oxygen-Level-Dependent (BOLD) signal — an indirect proxy for neural activity. When neurons fire, local blood flow increases, raising the ratio of oxygenated to deoxygenated hemoglobin, which increases the MR signal.

Hemodynamic Response Function (HRF)

The BOLD response peaks ~5-6 seconds after a neural event and returns to baseline over ~20 seconds. Adjust the parameters to see how conditions differ.

Expected Findings

Based on the literature, the Incongruent > Congruent contrast should reveal activity in regions associated with cognitive control and conflict monitoring:

ACC / dACC
Conflict monitoring; detects response competition
DLPFC
Top-down control; suppresses incorrect response
Inferior frontal
Response inhibition; stopping prepotent responses
Parietal cortex
Attention reorienting; top-down attentional control
